SDF from a mesh experimental

A procedural torus knot is baked in a Web Worker with bakeVoxelSdfInWorker. finalizeSdf integrates its mass and full inertia tensor, principalInertia diagonalises it, and world.enableSdfCollisions binds the field to each knot body. The same steps work for a glTF mesh: pass its geometry's positions and indices.

Limits (see SDF_WORLD.md):

  • Experimental and unpublished: the API may change.
  • Only collisions change. Mass and inertia stay those of the body's proxy box unless you pass massProperties (here from finalizeSdf + principalInertia).
  • You keep the field, body and render geometry in the same principal frame yourself.
  • The mesh must be closed and consistently wound; features smaller than a voxel are lost.
  • Adding or removing bodies needs await world.updateSdfCollisions() before the next step.

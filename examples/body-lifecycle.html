Body lifecycle (headless)

examples/body-lifecycle.ts walks one body through its life on a device the page owns: step it, setEnabled(false) to pause it with its GPU state retained, reset({ velocity }), enable and step again, then remove() it and add a sphere that reuses the slot under a new id.

There is no rendering: the page prints what the function returns. Expect resumed to be further along x than paused, and replacementId to differ from originalId. See BODY_LIFECYCLE.md.

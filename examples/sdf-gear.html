SDF gears experimental

One gear mesh profile is baked into a voxel SDF with bakeVoxelSdfAsync, and finalizeSdf integrates its mass and inertia. The gold input gear is kinematic (a fixed body driven with moveTo); the blue gear turns freely on a two-ball-joint axle and is pushed only by non-convex SDF tooth contacts.

The ideal ratio is −24/16 = −1.5. Expect a little less (about −1.4 in our Chrome run): the voxel tooth contacts slip slightly.

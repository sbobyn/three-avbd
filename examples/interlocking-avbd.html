Interlocking sculpture: AVBD SDF counterpart

Two pre-linked rings hang from a peg. Orbit to inspect the openings, press Play, then push the lower ring. No joint connects the rings: support comes from contact. This counterpart reuses the Rapier baseline inputs and rendering. No performance winner is established.

Ring shape: exact analytic torus fields, not convex pieces. The Rapier baseline approximates each ring with 16, 32 or 64.

Balls in a torus experimental

A fixed ring uses an analytic torus field (new AnalyticField({ kind: 'torus' })), so its hole is really empty. A convex hull of the same ring would fill it in. The balls and floor get analytic sphere and box fields automatically when SDF collisions are enabled.

The balls drop into the hole, which holds them: they settle on the floor inside the ring and stay there. Drop again moves them back with body.set(), which needs no SDF refresh.

Interlocking sculpture: Rapier baseline

Two pre-linked rings hang from a peg. Orbit to inspect the openings, press Play, then push the lower ring. No joint connects the rings: support comes from contact. This is a Rapier-first prototype, not an AVBD comparison result.

Initializing…

Fixed timestep 1/120 s; 10 Rapier solver iterations; sleeping and CCD off for this baseline. Changing piece count resets the scene, while mass, inertia and visible mesh stay fixed. Convex tube sectors approximate the surface: more pieces is a representation choice, not automatically better physics.

Observation export contains inputs, poses, settings and device/build context—not an editable scene or a benchmark. Study protocol.

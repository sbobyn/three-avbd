AVBD on WebGPU

Augmented Vertex Block Descent (Giles, Diaz & Yuksel, SIGGRAPH 2025), simulated entirely on your GPU in the browser: collision, graph colouring and the solve.

Drag blocks to pull them · B shoots a box · drag to orbit, scroll to zoom

This browser doesn't support WebGPU, so only the small scenes run, on the CPU. For the GPU scenes use a recent Chrome or Edge, or Safari 26 or later.

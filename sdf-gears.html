How it works

Only the gold gear is driven, kinematically: its angle is set each step, with no motor torque. The other gears rotate freely on two ball-joint axle constraints. Their motion comes from non-convex SDF tooth contacts, with no gear-ratio constraint or scripted follower angles. Colors show the relative normal force at sampled solver contacts. Involute profiles are baked to resolution-80 voxel fields; mass and inertia are integrated from the extruded polygons. This is an experimental collision demo, not a precision mechanical simulator.
